1. General Information & Scope of Application

Data Protection Overview

The following information provides a simple overview of what happens to your personal data when you visit our website or use our Android applications ("Dot Hub for Glyph Matrix" and "N Clock"). Personal data is any data that can be used to personally identify you.

Scope of Application

This privacy policy applies to the use of:

  • The website operated by nostream digital solutions.
  • The Android app "Dot Hub for Glyph Matrix".
  • The Android app "N Clock".
  • The in-app Webviews integrated within these apps that load our website content.

Important Distinction: Our website operates entirely without cookies, marketing analytics, or tracking technologies. Similarly, our apps are designed for data economy, generally operating offline without connecting to our own servers, utilizing no tracking, no profiling, no personalized advertising, and no crashlytics services. However, technical data processing still occurs automatically via server log files when you visit the site or load a Webview within the apps, when you proactively contact us via e-mail, or when the apps communicate with Google Play services for license/subscription verification.

3. Website & Webview Data Processing

Hosting & Server Log Files

Our website (and by extension, the website content loaded via Webviews in our apps) is hosted by STRATO AG, Otto-Ostrowski-Straße 7, 10249 Berlin, Germany ("Strato").

When you access our website or open a Webview in the apps, Strato automatically collects and stores information in so-called server log files, which your browser or app automatically transmits to the server. These are:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and browser version
  • Operating system used
  • Referrer URL
  • Time of the server request

This data is not merged with other data sources. The collection of this data is based on Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR. The operator has a legitimate interest in the technically error-free presentation and optimization of their services – for this purpose, the server log files must be recorded.

  • Recipients of the data: Our hosting provider Strato processes this data on our behalf. We have concluded a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) with Strato in accordance with Art. 28 GDPR.
  • Storage duration: IP addresses are stored anonymously by Strato for a maximum of 7 days to detect and ward off attacks. After that, they are deleted unless legal retention obligations require longer.
  • Obligation to provide data: Not legally or contractually required, but technically necessary for the service and functionality of our website and Webviews.

SSL/TLS Encryption

For security reasons and to protect the transmission of confidential content, this site uses SSL or TLS encryption. You can recognize an encrypted connection by the fact that the address line changes from "http://" to "https://" and by the lock symbol in your browser line.

4. App Functions and Data Processing

In the apps ("Dot Hub for Glyph Matrix" and "N Clock"), only information that is technically necessary to provide the respective function is processed. Personal data is not transmitted to us as the controller nor to third parties (except for the Google Play billing/licensing process).

Shared App Features

  • Settings: UI settings (e.g., Dark Mode, clock faces) to maintain your preferred app configurations. Local storage only within the app's storage on the device. No transmission.
  • Notifications & Alarms: Optional, purely local notifications of the app (e.g., alarms, timers, reminders). Managed by the device's OS. No push tokens or IDs are sent to servers.

App-Specific Features

  • Visualizer (Dot Hub only): Real-time audio processing in the RAM to display visualizations matching the current audio signal. Processing exclusively in RAM, without permanent storage. No recording or transmission of audio data takes place.

5. Google Play Subscriptions & Licensing

While the apps do not transmit data to our own servers, they require a technical connection to Google Play services for purchase verification (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR):

  • Subscriptions (Dot Hub): Verifying active subscriptions ("Dot Hub Pro" / "Support") to unlock features via the Google Play Billing Library.
  • Purchase Verification (N Clock): Verifying your one-time purchase to authorize app usage via the Google Play Licensing service.

Data Disclosure & Third-Country Transfer: License verification and subscription status are processed directly between your device and Google's servers. No financial or account data is stored by us. Google processes transaction and licensing data as an independent controller. No transmission to third countries is carried out by us as the controller. Please note that Google may process transaction and licensing data on servers located in the United States. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (DPF), which ensures an adequate level of data protection in accordance with GDPR standards. Details can be found in Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Contacting Us by E-Mail

If you contact us by e-mail, your inquiry including all resulting personal data (e.g., name, e-mail address, content of your request) will be stored and processed by us for the purpose of handling your request.

  • Legal Basis: Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. b GDPR (if related to a contract or pre-contractual measures) or Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR (legitimate interest in effective processing of inquiries).
  • Recipients: Your data is received directly by us. Depending on our technical infrastructure, our e-mail hosting provider may act as a processor.
  • Storage Duration: Data remains with us until the purpose lapses (e.g., conclusively processed), you request deletion, or revoke consent, subject to mandatory statutory retention periods.
  • Obligation: Voluntary, but an e-mail address and inquiry context are necessary for us to respond.

7. Storage Period & Deletion (App Data)

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete all data stored by the apps at any time by uninstalling the app or by clearing the app storage or cache in your device's system settings.
