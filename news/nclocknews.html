CB 0.8: What's New

2026-06-11

N Clock Change Log.

Closed Beta 8 (CB 0.8)

A brand new home screen, your phone's own alarm sounds, and the easiest way yet to add widgets.

A New Home Screen

  • Fresh Design: The home screen now scrolls, with a large clock in the middle, today's date and a tidier news section.
  • Clear Shortcuts: Alarms, Timer, Stopwatch and Settings each get their own card with a short description, so everything is one tap away.
  • Widget Showcase: Browse all available widgets right inside the app and add them to your home screen with a single tap.

More Sounds

  • System Sounds: You can now use your phone's built-in alarm sounds. Look for the new "System Sounds" album in the song picker.
  • New Ringtones: Eight new alarm sounds have been added, and the tracks previously marked "Coming Soon" are now ready to play.

Languages

  • Fully Translated: All new features are available in German and Hindi.

Closed Beta 7 (CB 0.7)

Light mode arrives, and the home screen gets quick-access shortcuts.

Pick Your Theme

  • Light, Dark or System: Choose your favourite look in Settings, or let the app follow your phone's setting automatically.
  • Everything Adapts: The whole app, including the analog clock and progress rings, switches colors to match your chosen theme.

Home Screen Shortcuts

  • Quick Access: New cards on the clock screen take you straight to Timer, Alarm, Stopwatch and Settings.

Closed Beta 6 (CB 0.6)

Widgets for your home screen, two new languages and a much smaller download.

Widgets

  • Six Widgets: Analog Clock, Big Clock, Next Alarm (shows "Today" or "Tomorrow"), Live Timer with a countdown ring, Quick Start Timer with one-tap presets, and a Stopwatch you can start, lap and reset right from your home screen.

New Languages

  • German and Hindi: N Clock is now fully translated, including the legal pages.
  • Per-App Language: On Android 13 and newer you can set the app's language independently of your phone's language.

Smaller & Smarter

  • Much Smaller Download: The app shrank from about 163 MB to about 12 MB. The large high-quality ringtones download automatically in the background shortly after install.
  • Alarms Always Ring: If a ringtone hasn't finished downloading yet, a built-in backup sound plays instead. You'll never miss an alarm.
  • Stopwatch Memory: The stopwatch now remembers your time and laps, even after you close the app.

Closed Beta 5 (CB 0.5)

Better timers, a smoother feel and the first home screen widgets.

Timer & Stopwatch

  • Redesigned Timer Setup: A new picker for hours, minutes and seconds, plus quick presets for common durations.
  • Instant Start: Timers now start without any delay.
  • Live Notifications: On Android 16, the timer notification shows a live progress bar.
  • Stopwatch Side Bar: Play, reset and lap controls are now easier to reach.

A Cleaner Clock Screen

  • Perfectly Centered Clock: The digital clock now sits right in the middle of your screen, where it belongs.
  • Smoother News Cards: Fixed an issue where news cards could sometimes show up as a black box. They now load reliably every time.

Polish

  • Smooth Animations: Switching between tabs now slides smoothly.
  • Notification Reminder: The app warns you when notifications are turned off, so alarms and timers can always reach you.

Closed Beta 4 (CB 0.4)

A brand new way to pick alarm sounds, and a guide to keep alarms reliable.

The Song Picker

  • Sounds Organised by Artist and Album: Choosing an alarm sound is no longer a long dropdown list. Sounds are now neatly sorted into albums, just like your favourite music app.
  • Listen Before You Choose: Tap any sound to preview it before setting it as your alarm. No more surprises in the morning.
  • Friendly Sound Names: Your alarm list now shows the actual name of each sound, instead of a confusing file name.
  • Four New Sounds: Added two new alert tones and two new melodic tones, each with their own album cover.

Reliability

  • Setup Guide: A new screen explains how to stop your phone from quietly killing alarms overnight, with tips for Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Honor and Nothing OS.
  • Safer Restarts: Alarms are restored more reliably after a phone restart.
  • Faster Alarm List: Tapping an alarm reacts quicker, and day labels now match your language.

Closed Beta 3 (CB 0.3)

A new look and sturdier alarms.

New Look

  • Floating Navigation Bar: A pill-shaped navigation bar with smooth animations replaces the standard one.
  • News Card: The clock screen now shows the latest N Clock news.

Sturdier Alarms

  • Snooze Keeps Your Sound: When you snooze an alarm, it rings again with the same ringtone and label you chose.
  • Crash Prevention: The app no longer crashes if alarm permissions are missing on Android 12 and newer.
  • Clear Error Messages: If an alarm can't be scheduled, the app now tells you instead of failing silently.

Closed Beta 2 (CB 0.2)

Alarms that survive a phone restart.

Reliability

  • Ring After Reboot: Alarms now ring after a phone restart — even before you unlock the phone for the first time.
  • Smarter Repeats: Daily and weekly alarms reschedule themselves automatically, and one-time alarms switch themselves off after ringing.
  • Battery Friendly: A ringing alarm now stops after 10 minutes instead of 2 hours, saving battery.

Timer

  • Controls in Notifications: Stop or reset a running timer right from the notification, and dismiss the notification while the timer keeps running.

Closed Beta 1 (CB 0.1)

The very first closed beta of N Clock.

Getting Started

  • Welcome Screen: A friendly first-launch experience that asks for notification permission where needed.
  • Legal Pages: Terms of Service, Imprint and Privacy Policy are available right inside the app.
  • Feedback Button: Send feedback by email straight from Settings, with helpful device info attached automatically.
