Dot Hub 4.0 Open Beta 1 – Now on the Play Store

Dot Hub 4.0 Open Beta 1 is live. Join the open beta on Google Play and try everything that's new:

Dot Matrix widget

The Glyph Matrix now lives on your home screen. Pick an animation for each widget, and decide what a tap does: open the app, or start a live 30 second Ultimate AOD session right on the launcher.

Animation editor

Build your own matrix animations frame by frame. Draw and erase with three brush sizes, set brightness, drop in circles and squares, then mirror, flip, rotate, invert or shift a frame. Copy, delete and reorder frames, with 30 steps of undo and redo.

Share your designs

Turn any animation into a link. Whoever opens it sees a preview first, then imports it straight into their own library.

NClock Glyph Toy

A new analog clock for the matrix, in a detailed and a minimal style. Unlocks with NClock installed or with Dot Hub Pro.

Music visualizer switch in Ultimate AOD

The visualizer is now its own toggle in Ultimate AOD, and it handles changing or dropped audio sessions far more reliably.

New look

Redesigned home screen with a short description for every toy, new Geist typography, and a floating frosted glass navigation bar.
