N Clock: Coming Soon_

Time, reimagined.

N Clock is a simple clock app. Inspired by the Nothing design language, it provides a clean and highly functional experience in one straightforward dark theme interface.

Features

  • Minimalist Design: Experience time without distractions. NClock follows the iconic dot-matrix aesthetic.
  • Nothing OS Integration: Designed to fit perfectly into your Nothing ecosystem.

Status

The app is currently in the final stages of development and will be available on the Google Play Store soon. Stay tuned for more updates!
