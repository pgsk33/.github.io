N Clock is Out Now!_

Download on Google Play

Time, reimagined.

N Clock is a simple clock app. Inspired by the Nothing design language, it provides a clean and highly functional experience in one straightforward dark theme interface.

Features

  • Minimalist Design: Experience time without distractions. NClock follows the iconic Nothing aesthetic.
  • Nothing OS Integration: Designed to fit perfectly into your Nothing ecosystem.
  • Sound Packs: 4 sound packs tailored to Nothing OS.
  • And more: Learn more on the N Clock page.

Status

N Clock is now available on the Google Play Store! Download it today and start using it right away.
