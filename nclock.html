NClock.

A simple clock app.

N Clock is a simple clock app for Android. Alarms, timer and stopwatch in one clean dark interface, inspired by the Nothing design language.

No ads. No tracking. No subscription. Buy it once, it stays yours.

Download on Google Play
Important: Allow background usage on Nothing OS

Built to wake you up.

An alarm has one job. It has to ring. N Clock treats that as the headline, not a side note.

Alarms that actually ring

Exact alarm scheduling on every supported Android version. Direct Boot support, so your alarms still trigger after a reboot, even before you unlock the phone. The full screen alarm wakes the display and plays through Do Not Disturb. Snooze or stop from the lock screen with a single tap.

There is also a setup guide for Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Honor and Nothing OS, so the system stops quietly killing your alarms overnight.

Features.

Alarms

Labels, daily or one-off repeats, your own tones. A growing library of hand-made and licensed ringtones.

Timer

Quick presets for tea (Black, Green, Chai, Oolong) and common durations. A notification you can see at a glance, a plus one minute button and instant restart. Keeps running after you close the app or reboot the phone.

Stopwatch

Lap times, clear numbers, nothing else in the way.

Clock

One focused dark theme. Nothing inspired red on black.

Privacy by design.

  • No analytics, no crash reporters, no advertising IDs
  • No account, no sign in, no cloud sync
  • Works fully offline
  • Your data stays on your device
  • GDPR-compliant, built in Germany

The only time the app talks to the internet is when Google Play checks the license for your one time purchase.

Requirements.

  • Android 12 or newer

On the first launch, N Clock asks for three things:

  • Notifications
  • Schedule exact alarms
  • Display over the lock screen, so the full screen alarm can appear
