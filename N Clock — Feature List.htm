de.nostream.nclock · Android 12+ N Clock CB 0.9 A clock app inspired by the Nothing design language — alarms, timers, stopwatch, and home-screen widgets built for one thing above all: reliability. Single-activity Jetpack Compose, no DI framework, no destructive migrations, works on every Android version since 12. 5 Main screens 6 Home widgets 19+ Alarm sounds 4 Themes 3 Languages Alarms Full alarm clock with exact scheduling that survives reboots, doze, and process death. Create, edit, delete alarms with custom labels and per-alarm ringtone. Recurrence: one-time, daily, or any custom set of weekdays — next-occurrence logic computed per alarm. Exact scheduling via AlarmManager.setAlarmClock(), immune to battery optimization deferral. Full-screen alarm UI over the lock screen with Stop and Snooze (5 minutes). 10-minute auto-timeout so a missed alarm never rings forever. Reschedules after reboot — handles both BOOT_COMPLETED and LOCKED_BOOT_COMPLETED, so alarms are re-armed even before first unlock. Device-protected storage for the alarm database: alarms fire even if the phone restarted overnight and was never unlocked. Permission gate before saving: checks exact-alarm permission and battery optimization, with a deliberate hold-to-continue escape hatch and a visible warning when notifications are disabled. Foreground service playback with wake lock — ringtone keeps playing with the app swiped away. Timer A saved-timer library rather than a single countdown — state lives outside the UI process. Saved timers with categories — Recent, Tea, and Common — shown as tappable cards with live progress. Custom timer dialog with hour/minute/second pickers, labels, and quick preset chips. Foreground countdown service with a persistent notification updated every second. Survives process death: timer state is persisted to DataStore and recovered by the service on restart. Full-screen finish alert with Restart, +1 minute, and Stop actions. Active-timer header in the app showing the running countdown with one-tap stop. Stopwatch Start, pause, reset with centisecond display and lap recording. Wall-clock based timing persisted to DataStore — the stopwatch keeps counting through app kills and reboots. Companion home-screen widget with responsive layouts and direct start/pause control. Home / Clock screen Digital and analog clock rendered live in Compose. Feature shortcuts — quick tiles into Timer, Alarm, Stopwatch, and Settings. Widget discovery row: browse all six widgets in-app and pin them to the launcher with one tap (requestPinAppWidget, with graceful fallback on unsupported launchers). News card — embedded WebView surfacing N Clock news, tap-through to the full page. Home-screen widgets Widget What it shows Big Clock Large digital time for the home screen. Stopwatch Elapsed time with start/pause from the widget. Sound library Alarm sounds organized like a music app — albums, artists, cover art — plus everything the device already ships with. Album-based picker with cover art and in-place preview playback before choosing. Built-in catalog: NC Melodic and NC Alert (N Clock), ccr singles (ccr_beats), daversity topic (Daversity) — 19 curated tracks. On-demand ringtone pack via Play Asset Delivery: 12 tracks download only when wanted, with a banner showing progress, waiting-for-Wi-Fi, and failure states. System sounds album: the device's stock alarm tones appear as one extra album, loaded in the background. Spotify artist links — "Listen on Spotify" for featured artists. Guaranteed fallback: if a chosen track can't resolve (pack not downloaded), the alarm still rings with a bundled default. Themes & design Four theme modes: System, Dark, Light, and QuintEssential — white with a yellow accent. Nothing design language: dot-matrix-inspired type, red accent, 20 dp rounded containers. Theme-aware system bars — status and navigation bars follow the selected mode. Animated navigation: directional slide transitions between the five bottom-nav tabs. Localized in English, German, and Hindi. Legal pages in-app: Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Imprint. Onboarding & reliability Every error can have severe consequences — the first-run flow exists to make alarms un-killable. Four-step onboarding: Welcome → Notification permission → Background/battery permission → Reliability tips. Runtime permission handling for notifications (Android 13+), exact alarms (Android 12+), and full-screen intents (Android 14+). Reliability tips screen explaining battery optimization and OEM-specific pitfalls. Smart in-app review: Play review prompt gated on real usage — app opens, dismissed alarms and completed timers as satisfaction signals, capped asks, and cooldown after "Not now". Under the hood Area Implementation Architecture Single-activity MVVM, Jetpack Compose, manual dependency wiring — no DI framework. Persistence Room v4 with explicit migrations (never destructive) in device-protected storage; DataStore for preferences and timer/stopwatch state. Services Foreground AlarmService and TimerService with redelivered intents, so audio and countdowns outlive the UI. Notifications Dedicated channels: alarm (high), running timer (low, persistent), timer finished (max, full-screen intent). Compatibility Min SDK 31 (Android 12) through target SDK 35 — tested behavior on every version in between. Delivery Play Asset Delivery for on-demand ringtones; Play In-App Review.
